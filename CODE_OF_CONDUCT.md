# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person. Assume the other party is
competent and acting in good faith until you have real evidence otherwise.

## What is expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and useful. Personal attacks, harassment,
  demeaning comments and unwelcome attention are not.
- Critique the work: "this scoring change regresses Chinese search, here is the
  case" rather than "this is bad".
- English and 中文 are both fine in issues and PRs. Do not make anyone feel
  unwelcome for their fluency in either.
- Assume the maintainer is a person with other obligations. A reply may take days.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing others'
private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers privately.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject
contributions, and may ban accounts for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

This is adapted from the spirit, if not the letter, of the Contributor Covenant.
